# GDPR implementation notes

The existing privacy, cookies, terms, consent runtime, account-data export/deletion architecture remain in place.

The course-engine foundation adds only learning activity/progress data. Do not collect immigration status, diagnoses, legal case details or other sensitive data to personalize lessons. Work, Kela, Migri, healthcare and banking scenarios are language practice only.

Before production, configure the real controller name, privacy contact, retention criteria and processor/subprocessor documentation.
