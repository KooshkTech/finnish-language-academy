# OpiOpe V24.12 — Notifications, assignment status and deadlines

## Added
- In-app notifications for students and teachers.
- New assignment → student notification.
- New student submission → teacher notification.
- Teacher feedback → student notification.
- 48-hour due-soon reminders for students who have not submitted.
- Overdue reminders for students who have not submitted.
- Student dashboard deadline states: open, due soon, overdue, submitted, reviewed.
- Teacher assignment controls: open, close, archive, reopen.
- Teacher submission summary: submitted, waiting for review, missing, deadline status.
- Missing-student list on each assignment.
- Class assignment cards show submission/review/missing counts.

## Database migration
Apply:
`supabase/migrations/20260914100000_assignment_notifications.sql`

## Scheduled reminders
Configure `CRON_SECRET` and schedule:
`GET /api/cron/assignment-reminders`
with header:
`Authorization: Bearer $CRON_SECRET`

The route is idempotent through per-user notification source keys.
