# OpiOpe V22.1 — Speaking Conversations

Added structured speaking conversation practice to every CEFR level A0–C2.

## Coverage
- A0: first meeting, café, asking where something is
- A1: shop, booking an appointment, neighbour small talk
- A2: work phone call, housing problem, weekend story
- B1: job interview, complaint, opinion and justification
- B2: workload negotiation, disagreement in a meeting, demanding customer situation
- C1: expert discussion, public panel discussion, constructive feedback
- C2: implied meaning and nuance, rhetorical debate, register switching

Each level contains 3 scenario-based conversations with 6 dialogue turns, learner prompts, useful phrases, Finnish browser TTS controls (normal and slow), role selection, optional English support, and microphone recording/replay through the existing SpeakingRecorder.

No pronunciation score is fabricated. A real speech-analysis provider is still required before pronunciation scoring can be shown.
